Our Process

How we bring your vision to life

01

Discovery

We start by understanding your business, goals, and target audience to establish a solid foundation for your project.

02

Research & Strategy

We research the market, analyze competitors, profile the audience, and define a clear creative direction to guide the design.

03

Concept Development

We develop initial design concepts based on strategy and inspiration, refine them internally, and prepare them for client review.

04

Client Presentation

We present the concepts with clear explanations, gather client feedback, and align on the preferred creative direction.

05

Revisions & Refinement

We revise the selected concept, fine-tuning details like color, typography, and layout to polish the final design.

06

Final Approval

We submit the completed design for client approval, making any minor final adjustments if needed.

07

Production & Delivery

We prepare final files in all necessary formats and deliver them securely to the client.

08

Post-Delivery Support

We offer a support window for minor edits and assist the client in using their new assets effectively.

09

Project Closing

We close the project with final invoicing, request feedback, and create case studies to showcase the work.
