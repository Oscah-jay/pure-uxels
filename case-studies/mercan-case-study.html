Mercan AutoSpares

UI/UX Design for Automotive Spare Parts Platform

Project Overview

Mercan AutoSpares is a digital platform connecting buyers and sellers of automotive spare parts. We designed a clean, intuitive UI/UX that simplifies navigation, streamlines purchasing, and enhances the overall digital experience for users.

The project focused on creating a user-friendly interface with clear information architecture, responsive design, and a cohesive visual identity aligned with the automotive industry.

Services

  • UI Design
  • UX Design
  • Web App Design
  • Interaction Design

Year

2025

Industry

Automotive / E-Commerce

The Challenge

Mercan AutoSpares needed a digital platform that would make browsing, comparing, and purchasing automotive parts easy and efficient. The challenge was to design an interface that works seamlessly across devices while remaining visually appealing and user-centric.

The Solution

We developed a modern UI with intuitive navigation, smart filtering options, and a visually structured layout. The UX design prioritizes speed, clarity, and accessibility, ensuring users can find and purchase parts quickly while enjoying a consistent brand experience.
